# Code of conduct

This repository is small and mostly maintained by one person, but issues and pull requests are open
to anyone. The rules are short, in the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good faith, including when you disagree.
- Keep feedback about the work, not about the person.
- Accept that a change may be declined, and say why when you decline one.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal attacks, trolling or deliberate disruption.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.

## Reporting

If something here makes you uncomfortable, report it privately through GitHub's
[private reporting form](https://github.com/zxxdev/zxxdev.github.io/security/advisories/new), and
start the title with "Conduct". Reports are read by the maintainer only and are kept confidential.

## What happens next

Depending on how serious it is: a private warning, removal of the comment or contribution, or a
block from the repository. The decision is explained to the person reporting and, where it is safe,
to the person reported.
